# Tidecaller — a duel of shells and tides

An original turn-based card duel on a moonlit lagoon. You are a tidecaller of
the South Shore; Nixie calls from the North Reef. Each turn refills your shells,
you draw a card, and you play creatures and spells from your hand. Creatures
strike at the end of your turn — the foe's guards soak the damage first and the
rest hits their life. Two cards of one tide in a turn: the second gains +1.
First tidecaller to 0 life loses the lagoon.

Play it: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or serve `game/` with any
static server. No network, no build step. Click a card to pick it up, click
again (or press Enter) to play it. Keys 1–7 pick a card, Space ends your turn,
R restarts, M mutes.
